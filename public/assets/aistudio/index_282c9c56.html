About SafeConnect

A centralized emergency alert system that connects you instantly with your trusted contacts and the authorities.

Help should never be more than a tap away

In an emergency, every second counts. SafeConnect brings every channel of help into one place. A single tap sends your alert and live location to the people who can respond, so you don’t have to call, type or explain while you’re in danger.

Every account is verified with a one-time password, so alerts come from real people and reach real responders.
